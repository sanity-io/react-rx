# Simple

The smallest possible start: read an observable with [`useObservable`](/reference#useobservable) and render its latest value. No effects, no subscriptions to manage, no cleanup.

## Hello World

`of('World')` emits synchronously, so the value is there on the very first render — no initial flash, no extra re-render after mount.

<HelloWorld />

## Counter

A `timer` drives the count. The hook subscribes for you and tears down when the component unmounts.
